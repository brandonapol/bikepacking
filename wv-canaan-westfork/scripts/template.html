Canaan + West Fork Loop

Two bikepacking.com routes stitched into one loop from the Alpena lot on US-33. Friday and Saturday ride the Canaan Valley & Forks of Cheat loop, Sunday rides the West Fork loop. Tap any step to see it on the map.

    Good to know

    No cell service

    Most of the route has none. Download offline maps in onX and Ride with GPS before leaving Dayton, and load the GPX on both phones.

    Camping rules

    Dispersed camping is fine on Monongahela NF land 100+ ft from roads, trails and rec sites. Not in state parks or on private land. Parsons, Thomas and Davis are mixed ownership, so Friday night means the Blackwater Falls SP campground, Canaan Valley SP or a motel.

    Water

    Rivers everywhere on Friday and Saturday; filter it. On Sunday the Middle Mountain hand pump at mile 34.5 is the only potable water. Bear Heaven has none.

    FR 209

    Not on this route. FR 209 runs off US-250 near Cheat Bridge, about 20 mi upstream of the Shavers Fork miles we ride. Its bridge closure ended Aug 14, 2025. Check MNF alerts for anything new.

    Saturday night

    We end Saturday at the car. Bear Heaven is a 4 mi drive up FR 91, so drive up, camp, and drive back down Sunday morning.

    Hunting season

    Archery deer season runs through the fall in WV. A blaze orange vest or helmet cover is cheap insurance.
